What the model tells you

Zen 4 and Zen 5 use separate, explicitly selected models and pinned exact-form measurements. This workbench exposes the experimental resource and register-recurrence analyzer.

Read the two bounds separately

The resource bound is conditional on the stated execution-resource and occupancy assumptions. The recurrence bound describes loop-carried register dependencies. Taking their maximum does not produce a complete pipeline prediction.

Missing is not zero

“Unavailable” means the data or model is incomplete. Memory, aliasing, forwarding, front-end delivery, finite queues, retirement and some wide or masked forms remain unmodeled. Neither target is hardware-qualified.

Scope of the input

One repeated straight-line block, with ideal register renaming. Alignment effects and branch prediction are not modeled. Uploaded bytes are decoded, never executed. Assembly input accepts instructions and labels in Intel syntax.
